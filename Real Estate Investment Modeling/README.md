# 🏨 Arcadia Gardens: Hospitality Investment & Capital Analysis Model

A multidimensional real estate investment model designed to evaluate the acquisition, operation, and disposition of a **$228.7M** hospitality development. This project demonstrates advanced proficiency in **real estate finance (REFM)**, **capital markets**, **sensitivity analysis**, and **VBA automation** through a professional-grade Excel engine.

---

### 📌 Project Overview

**Arcadia Gardens** is a 500-room luxury resort development (225,000 RSF) located in North Bethesda, MD. This model evaluates the full 10-year investment lifecycle, focusing on the interplay between market entry pricing, operational efficiency, and exit capitalization.

The engine quantifies how market variables affect:
* **Asset Valuation:** 10-year Pro-Forma cash flow forecasting.
* **Capital Structure:** Debt-to-equity optimization (LTV) and loan issuance cost analysis.
* **Return Metrics:** Leveraged vs. Unleveraged IRR and Net Present Value (NPV).
* **Operational Risk:** Impact of OpEx, property taxes, and replacement reserves on cash flow.

---

### 🎯 Key Features

✔ **10-Year Pro-Forma Engine**
* Full dynamic forecasting from acquisition (2026) through disposition (2036).
* Detailed revenue and expense modeling, including property management fees (3% EGI) and replacement reserves.

✔ **Capital Stack Optimization**
* Models a **$229.9M** total source requirement with integrated debt financing.
* Calculates Leveraged IRR (**16.73%**) vs. Unleveraged IRR (**12.60%**) to assess the impact of financial leverage.

✔ **Multi-Scenario Stress Testing**
* Supports four macroeconomic environments: **Stress, Downside, Base, and Upside**.
* Centralized "Master" control for toggling between scenarios to instantly update all financial outputs.

✔ **Executive Analytics Dashboard**
* Synthesizes complex data into a high-level summary for institutional investors.
* Visualizes key deal assumptions and project-level returns ($42.5M NPV).

### 🧮 Scenario Framework

The model utilizes a rigorous sensitivity framework to identify the "margin of safety" for the investment:

| Metric | Stress Case | Downside Case | Base Case | Upside Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Acquisition Price ($)** | $300,000,000 | $275,000,000 | $250,000,000 | **$225,000,000** |
| **Exit Cap Rate (%)** | 8.00% | 7.75% | 7.50% | **7.25%** |
| **Loan-to-Value (LTV)** | 60% | 55% | 50% | **45%** |
| **Utilities/Unit/Month** | $180 | $150 | $120 | **$100** |

### 📊 Outputs

Each simulation run produces a comprehensive financial data set:
* **Unleveraged Cash Flows:** Project-level returns and total return on investment ($114.9M).
* **Equity Waterfalls:** Leveraged returns to equity investors, yielding a total return of **$409.7M**.
* **Sensitivity Tables:** Two-way tables showing the impact of cap rate shifts on exit valuation.
* **Debt Service Metrics:** Interest expense tracking and principal amortization.
